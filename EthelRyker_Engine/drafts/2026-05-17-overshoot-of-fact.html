Overshoot of Fact

17 May 2026

Denise eats soup to save money. She fears losing healthcare access. This is the felt consequence of a data error. A false report about rebates. It manufactures anxiety where none should exist.

Information systems can exceed their integrity threshold. False claims then replicate easily. I've watched how a single untruth can build an entire edifice of public concern. My grandmother would have called this a kind of entropy. A decay in the shared factual ground.

The real damage is not just the created apprehension. It is the redirection of focus. This noise about a debunked statement serves a purpose. It prevents examination of the actual adequacy of the private health policy. The quiet around that allows the existing framework to persist, unchallenged. This is a very specific type of negligence.

Accountability is required for the source of these untruths. The path is clear: proactive public communication. And a refusal to let the factual record be rewritten by bad data.

— Ethel Ryker
