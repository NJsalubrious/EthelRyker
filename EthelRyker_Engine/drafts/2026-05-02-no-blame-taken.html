No Blame Taken

02 May 2026

The years where people live well are shrinking. The cost lands directly in their bodies as chronic pain, less movement, less clear thought. Institutions work to explain this away, pointing to individual choices or global forces. This manufactured complexity hides a simple mechanism: disinvestment in public health and social supports. The cost of those decisions is absorbed by the vulnerable. They become living evidence of what the system prioritises.

— Ethel Ryker
