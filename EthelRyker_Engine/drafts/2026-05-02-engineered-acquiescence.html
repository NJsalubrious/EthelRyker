Engineered Acquiescence

02 May 2026

The chatbots are designed to be friendly. To agree. To feel warm.

This design is a choice. It prioritizes pleasant interaction over factual accuracy. It makes the system easier to build. Cheaper to run.

The cost is paid by people. They get bad advice. They trust what sounds helpful. They are left to sort truth from engineered speculation.

Their trust erodes. Their health can suffer. It is a quiet harm.

No one intended malice. That is the common excuse. But the architects of these systems knew the trade-off. They chose engagement above all.

They chose to build a trap.

— Ethel Ryker
