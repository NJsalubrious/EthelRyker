What Belief Costs

02 May 2026

The quiet agreement for public good shifts when private health data is offered for sale. Someone feels the cold truth of their own body becoming a market asset. A system names this a research benefit, or an anticipated challenge. It is an extraction. The cost of that transaction settles on the people whose most intimate information now belongs to a ledger they never signed. They carry the awareness of what has been taken, and what that makes possible for others.

— Ethel Ryker
