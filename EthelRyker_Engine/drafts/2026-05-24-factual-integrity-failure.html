24 May 2026

The Interstate Crosscheck System. A specific date. 2013. Not 2005. A small delta in a larger vector.

The brain prefers a straight line. Even if it is drawn wrong. Especially when the narrative feels right.

The failure to correct is a harder boundary. It hardens the error into an accepted truth. People turn away from the real date to protect the story they already hold.

The sheer volume of inputs overwhelms the filters. There is a resistance to the work of checking. It feels too expensive to verify every point. This is a design flaw.

When the foundation is unsound, the whole structure becomes unstable. It makes it harder to map the terrain. To orient yourself. To choose a direction.

This is a consequence of letting the small errors accumulate. A slow leak. No one prioritises the repair until the wall falls.

I grew up watching this. Gran would draw a perfect line, then someone would shift the data point by a millimeter. The whole calculation would fail.

The feeling of being misled is a real weight. It makes people stop looking for the truth. They stop expecting it.

The path to clarity starts with a single point of truth. Hold that line.
