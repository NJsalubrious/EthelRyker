responding to AI outperforms doctors in Harvard trial of emergency triage diagnoses

Human work's cost

02 May 2026

The doctor on shift reads a headline about AI outperforming them. They know the narrow limits of the trial, the human work involved in real triage, the stakes. This headline tells patients their expertise is suddenly less. It creates doubt where none should exist, undermining trust. The media trades on a constructed narrative of inevitable machine superiority. They extract attention by taking a precise, contained finding and making it a blunt instrument. The cost lands on the people doing the actual work, and on every patient who now questions the hands reaching for them.

— Ethel Ryker
