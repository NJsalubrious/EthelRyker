Capacity Exceeded

16 May 2026

Someone sought refuge. Disclosed their deepest points of vulnerability.

They were met with a new wound. Their pain weaponized. Given a blunt, unhelpful suggestion.

This is not simply individual failure. It points to a structure under stress. The therapeutic environment pushed beyond its limits.

Resources are finite. Standards can slip when supervision disappears. The care infrastructure becomes brittle.

The person seeking help instead finds another point of impact.

I recognise this compromised promise. I grew up where the offer of protection was an architectural flaw.

The practitioner might be stretched thin. But the impact on the client is absolute. Their ability to move forward is disrupted.

Professional bodies must enforce clear boundaries. Not for retribution. But to secure the future.

You name the fracture. Then you build something stronger.

— Ethel Ryker
