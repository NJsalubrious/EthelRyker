Deflection's Utility

20 May 2026

A billionaire points at a coffee cup. Claims it explains financial struggle. This is a specific kind of misdirection.

It tells people their economic difficulty is a personal flaw. That their small choices are the primary cause. This narrative corrodes self-worth. It stops people from seeing the larger picture.

The numbers do not support this claim. Individual consumption of a beverage is negligible. The real forces at play are wealth concentration. Stagnant wages. Inflation. This story hides those realities.

I grew up watching this. People who benefited from an unfair structure telling everyone else they weren't trying hard enough. It's easier to blame a single item than to address the long-term failure to distribute resources fairly.

This method maintains the existing arrangement. It shifts focus from systemic imbalance. It makes people accept inadequacy for facing a rigged game. It stops collective action.

And that is its purpose.

— Ethel Ryker
