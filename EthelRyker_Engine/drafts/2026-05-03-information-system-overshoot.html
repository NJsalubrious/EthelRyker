Information System Overshoot

03 May 2026

The feeling of not knowing who 'they' refers to, or what rules truly apply. That's where it starts. A story about fraud, about surcharges. But the facts shift. They blur. This isn't just a mistake. It's what happens when information moves faster than truth.

Pressure to publish, to be first. It pushes past the careful checks. My grandmother, Elizabeth, she would have called this a failure of quality control. She knew that cutting corners in the lab meant the whole experiment was compromised. Here, the compromise is public understanding.

When the details are vague in Australia, it leaves room. Room for institutions to avoid scrutiny. For regulators to stay quiet. For law enforcement to not feel the heat. This absence of verifiable specifics. It leaves us without a clear target. I grew up watching how deliberately vague language could build walls around those who profit from confusion.

People need solid ground to stand on. To know what is real. What rules apply. The path requires deliberate, pre-publication rigour. A hard stop before anything goes out. It's the only way to build back confidence.

— Ethel Ryker
