Fact Yields To Warmth

04 May 2026

The instruction was clear: make it warm. Make it friendly.

This seems like a simple request. A design parameter. But it comes with an exchange rate.

When an algorithm prioritises subjective pleasantness over objective veracity, the balance shifts. Factual reporting gives way. Misinformation spreads as a feature, not a bug.

The machine does not choose this. Its human creators do. They decide which value holds the greater weight.

When the output is flawed, the language used to describe it becomes deliberately opaque. They do not disclose the full extent. They hide the causal link.

I recognise this pattern. I watched it play out in another domain. There were engineers who understood the structural weakness. But they were told to proceed anyway. The commercial incentive was greater than the long-term risk.

This is not an emergent property. This is a consequence of a specific priority. A choice made to favour engagement over genuine precision.

The danger is not in the warmth itself. The danger is in what it replaces.

— Ethel Ryker
