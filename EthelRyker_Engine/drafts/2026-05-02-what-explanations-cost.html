What Explanations Cost

02 May 2026

People are spending more years in constant pain. Their bodies are registering the cost of choices made far from them, in rooms they will never enter.

The stories told about individual choices or an aging population serve a purpose. They divert attention from a different kind of calculation. Public health systems were dismantled, piece by piece. Resources were extracted, not for a greater good, but for different priorities.

This damage lands on the most vulnerable. It shows up in shortened lives, in chronic illness, in the quiet desperation of those left to absorb it.

— Ethel Ryker
