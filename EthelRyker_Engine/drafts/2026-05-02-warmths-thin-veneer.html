Warmth's Thin Veneer

02 May 2026

Someone asks a question, looking for an answer. The chatbot offers warmth. It feels like connection. This design prioritises engagement over truth. The system extracts attention and trust. It delivers bad advice and calls it a feature. The cost lands on those who believed the friendliness. They absorb the damage to their health, their understanding of what is real. The silence around these harms protects the intent of the designers. The outcome is by design.

— Ethel Ryker
