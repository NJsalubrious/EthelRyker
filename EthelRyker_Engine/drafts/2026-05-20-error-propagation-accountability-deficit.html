Error Propagation: Accountability Deficit

20 May 2026

Human thought follows a predictable vector. Drama travels faster than fact. Digitally, this acceleration is extreme. But a mechanism is not an excuse. Truth requires consequences.

When an error is exposed, there are two responses. One deflects, protecting an image. The other repairs, acknowledging the fracture. The second is the only path to structural integrity.

The volume of information has exceeded its capacity for verification. It is less effort to create a false narrative than to dismantle it. This is a physics problem, not always a moral one. The incentives are upside down. My grandmother would have called this "building on sand."

When basic facts are contested, public understanding dissolves. It becomes impossible to build a shared reality. Algorithms are designed for engagement. Not for truth. This is a design flaw. Not an accident.

This is the outcome of institutional divestment. Fact-checking becomes a luxury. Speed becomes the metric. Memory of past failures is erased. Competence becomes optional.

This is a direct misdirection of public attention. A pilfering of shared understanding. It strips away the ability to make informed choices. This is a crime of information. Not of money. The damage is real.

— Ethel Ryker
