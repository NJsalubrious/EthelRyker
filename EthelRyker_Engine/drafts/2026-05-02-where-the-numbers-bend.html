Where The Numbers Bend

02 May 2026

People want to do the right thing for the planet, for their own health. They read headlines promising big changes with simple actions. But the numbers get stretched, just a little, to make a better story. This creates a pressure to perform impossible outcomes. It shifts the burden of systemic problems onto individual choices, making people feel like they aren't trying hard enough. The cost of this simplified narrative is absorbed by those trying to make sense of what is real, what actually works, and who is truly responsible.

— Ethel Ryker
