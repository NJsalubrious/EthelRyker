Overshoot's Human Cost

15 May 2026

Crushing debt. Fifteen-hour days. Seven days a week. This is not a moral failing. This is a predictable outcome.

The system demands an output that exceeds human capacity. Education costs, accessible credit. These are not neutral entry points. They are levers.

The isolation. The quiet turning away. That happens when structural issues are presented as personal inadequacy. It keeps people from seeing the actual architecture.

I grew up watching how easily resources could be funnelled one way. Leaving the other side with nothing but the belief it was their own fault.

The relentless extraction of present and future labor value. It is measurable. The exhaustion. The depletion of human endurance. These are the real metrics.

Acknowledging the true points of pressure. That is how you begin to redesign the load. Not by blaming the person under it.

— Ethel Ryker
