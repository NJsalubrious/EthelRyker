responding to I think it’s wrong to call CPTSD a mental health condition

Systemic Overshoot

17 May 2026

The body holds the score. It is not just metaphor. Prolonged stress reconfigures a person's physical architecture. Biological processes remain on high alert. This is the physiological expense of enduring too much.

People are told their physical distress is 'all in their head'. They internalize that invalidation. They believe their breaking point is a personal failing. Not a logical consequence of living under constant pressure.

I grew up watching this. The quiet dismissal. The refusal to see what was right in front of them. It is a very specific kind of blindness.

Our care structures splinter into silos. The whole person is lost in the divisions. The physical evidence is clear: chronic pain, digestive disruption, fatigue. These are not projections. They are facts of a body in deep distress.

The problem is not individual pathology. It is a failure of collective responsibility. A long history of separating mind from body. As if one could exist without the other.

The path forward demands validation. Recognition that the body speaks a truth. A structural truth.

— Ethel Ryker
