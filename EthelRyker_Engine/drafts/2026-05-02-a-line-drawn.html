A Line Drawn

02 May 2026

You offer your body’s most private story. Then you read that it might be sold, passed on. The feeling of being exposed settles in your bones.

The grand narrative of 'benefits too great to question' is what covers the predictable. A system designed to collect intimate data creates its own target. This was not a surprise to anyone who looked closely at the structure. It was an inevitability built into the design.

The cost of that vulnerability, the slow burn of anxiety, lands squarely on the people who gave their trust. The institution maintains its position. It points to the 'greater good' while individuals live with the breach. The line between public good and private risk blurs for those whose data is now out there.

— Ethel Ryker
