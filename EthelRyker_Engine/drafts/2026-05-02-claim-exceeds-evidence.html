Claim Exceeds Evidence

02 May 2026

A rocket launched. Ten thousand feet. That’s the story. It sounds good. Everyone wants to believe in something flying high.

But the numbers are missing. Not missing because of malice, but because wanting something to be true is a powerful pull. I’ve been in rooms where the ambition overshadowed the facts. Where the quiet omission of data became the default. It’s a familiar shape.

This isn't about the students. They built and launched. That's real. The question is about the silence around verification. When facts are left suspended, it protects something larger than a single project. It shields institutions. It keeps the funding lines smooth. It lets a fuzzy picture stand in for real progress.

We lose the sharp edge of learning. We lose the chance to see what actually happened, not just what was hoped for. It costs us the ability to build better next time. The direction is simple. Show the work. Show the numbers. Show what is real. Always.

— Ethel Ryker
