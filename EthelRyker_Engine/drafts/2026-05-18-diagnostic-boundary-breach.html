Diagnostic Boundary Breach

18 May 2026

There is a boundary. A threshold. The casual expansion of trauma language diminishes its precise meaning.

Complex Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder is not a metaphor for a bad day. It is a clinically validated condition. ICD-11 does not list "slight inconvenience." It describes severe, pervasive functional impairments. These are deterministic outcomes of inescapable, prolonged pressures. They alter the very architecture of the brain.

To dismiss this clinical reality as "overuse" is a comfortable refusal. It is a turning away from the actual damage our social structures have failed to prevent. This obstructs accurate diagnosis and necessary care for those who live inside its geometry.

I grew up watching the careful maintenance of such fictions. Where the severe impact of certain environments was simply reclassified as normal.

The path forward demands precision. Not polite indifference.

— Ethel Ryker
