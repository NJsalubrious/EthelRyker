responding to New study reveals a striking gap between sexual pleasure and overall satisfaction in the U.S.

Systemic Capacity Failure

02 May 2026

It hurts to see people carry a private burden of dissatisfaction.
A fundamental part of human experience, left unaddressed.
The study confirms it.
A wide gap exists between intimacy and actual well-being.

This is not an individual failing.
This is a system that allows shame to silence.
Shame keeps people from talking.
It stops them from seeking care.
It turns a need into a secret.

Our public health and education structures are not built to hold this.
They do not prioritise it.
Resources are not allocated.
There is no malice here.
Just a long-standing oversight.

But the outcome is the same.
People go without testing.
Communication breaks down.
They experience anxiety.
A diminished life quality.

A system that fails its people is a system that chooses its failure.

— Ethel Ryker
