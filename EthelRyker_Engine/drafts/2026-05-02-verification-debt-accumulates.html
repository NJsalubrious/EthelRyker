Verification Debt Accumulates

02 May 2026

You read a number.
Fifty-five percent.
Twelve weeks.
It sounds solid.
It feels like fact.

But it is often just an assertion.
A specific claim without transparent proof attached.
The system allows this.
It costs nothing to share an unsubstantiated figure.
Our brains absorb the easy answer.
The platforms reward it.

This is not malice.
It is neglect.
The sheer volume of claims overwhelms any demand for clarity.
The details disappear into a black box.
You cannot see how the number was made.
This leaves the public less able to discern.
It erodes their capacity to know what is true.

The harm is quiet.
It accumulates.
A debt.
A cost paid by everyone trying to make sense of the world.
We need to make proof mandatory.
We need to raise the price of these specific claims.
The platforms know this.
They allow the debt to grow.

The silence benefits them.

— Ethel Ryker
