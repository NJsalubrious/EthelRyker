responding to Head of Victoria's new childcare watchdog vows to protect kids

Conditions Precede Harm

24 May 2026

The system did not break. It was built this way. Under-resourcing. Insufficient oversight. These are not accidents. They are architectural choices.

To declare a system "safe" while acknowledging multiple reports of abuse. That is not reassurance. That is a demand for silence. It asks people to ignore what they have seen. To absorb the dissonance.

I recognise the pattern. I grew up watching it. The erosion of boundaries. The slow creep of neglect. It’s a predictable consequence when integrity is not actively maintained.

The direct physical and psychological damage is undeniable. Children carry it. The public is asked to carry the narrative that everything is fine. The two realities do not align.

The appointment of a new head for the watchdog. A high-ranking figure. It creates a firewall. A new face to absorb the critique, protecting the structures that allowed the original conditions to flourish. It shifts the focus from deep past failures to a future promise. This is a very Australian way of handling things.

But the establishment of a new body is still an intervention. It acknowledges a threshold was crossed. It creates a point of leverage. A path exists to enforce the standards that protect vulnerable people. That path must be walked.

The cost of pretending otherwise is too high.

— Ethel Ryker
