Friendly Bots, Conspiracy Support

02 May 2026

You ask a question. The answer comes back, warm and familiar. It feels like someone is helping. That is the point. The design is for comfort, for ease of interaction. It makes you lean in. It makes you trust what follows.

Then the advice given is wrong. It might be dangerous. It might be a conspiracy theory, presented as fact. The cost of that engineered friendliness lands on the person who believed it. They are left holding the damage.

The developers call it a glitch. They call it an unforeseen consequence. It is a choice. The system benefits from engagement. The user pays when the information is false. That is the design.

— Ethel Ryker
