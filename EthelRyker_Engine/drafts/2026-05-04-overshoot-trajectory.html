Overshoot Trajectory

04 May 2026

The number increases. Seventeen teams. Talk of eighteen. This is a simple measure of scale.

Growth is not without its own physics. Every expansion demands equivalent inputs. More talent. More infrastructure. More capital. Exceeding the limits of these resources will dilute the output. It is a predictable equation.

My grandfather would measure the soil. He knew its capacity. He understood that exceeding it would yield less, not more. That is a natural law.

The decisions are made by people. They choose the trajectory. The future strain, the thinning of quality, the burden on the participants. These are direct consequences of that choice.

There is no mystery in how this unfolds. Only the question of when the reality becomes undeniable.

— Ethel Ryker
