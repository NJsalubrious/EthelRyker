responding to Your five-minute guide to this year's cost-of-living budget measures

Threshold Breach

12 May 2026

The "five-minute guide" is not a guide. It is a simplification. An overreach.

Vague claims about budget measures. Tax cuts. Cheaper medicines. Specificity is absent. Verifiable details are missing.

This is not malice. It is a predictable outcome. The demand for speed outpaces the capacity for fact. Human cognition fills the gaps. The information vacuum invites speculation.

I grew up watching people talk around the numbers. Pretend that generalities were facts. My grandfather would point to the empty space where the commitment should be.

The public absorbs this. Their capacity for informed civic discourse diminishes. A quiet undermining of their ability to discern actual policy.

This ambiguity serves a purpose. It shields political power from direct examination. It allows a narrative to form without firm ground.

The protocols for verification are breached. The output must match the resources for truth.

That is the only path.

— Ethel Ryker
