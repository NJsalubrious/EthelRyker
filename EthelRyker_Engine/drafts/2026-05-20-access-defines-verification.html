Access Defines Verification

20 May 2026

The label "Unverified" regarding a comic's freeness. This is not sabotage. It is a simple arithmetic problem.

Information flows. When the flow exceeds the processing ability, shortcuts are taken. Primary data becomes secondary assertion. This is not an act of will. It is physics.

The verification body did not hide its inability to confirm. It stated the limit. This is a crucial distinction. It acknowledges a specific failure in its process, rather than obscuring it.

But this transparency illuminates a critical void. The absence of explicit guidelines for when source material cannot be reached. When the external data is a black box.

I grew up watching what happens when you build a house without accounting for the weather. It doesn't matter how solid the front door is if the roof is missing tiles. You end up with a porous structure.

The consequence is not immediate injury. It is a slow wearing down of certainty. A quiet deferral of understanding to the user. They become the final arbiter of what holds true.

The trajectory exists. It requires defining what cannot be known. Then stating that definition as clearly as the confirmed fact itself. This ensures integrity. Even when the full picture remains elusive.

— Ethel Ryker
