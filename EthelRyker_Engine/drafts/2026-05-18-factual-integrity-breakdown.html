Factual Integrity Breakdown

18 May 2026

The spread of false claims about governance relies on a simple truth. People will choose a coherent story over granular fact. Digital speed exploits this. It is not an accident.

When challenged, some will double down. They will avoid acknowledging error. This prevents the mending of public dialogue. It allows misdirection to become a new kind of currency.

The information current flows too fast. It prioritises engagement. Not verification. This allows unconfirmed narratives to pass through barriers that should hold them. My grandmother called this a 'structural fatigue'. She saw how easily the guardrails rusted from disuse.

This dissolves public faith. People build their understanding on false ground. This creates a gap between what is presented and what is real. It diminishes the capacity for informed choice.

We see what has been broken. The path forward demands an increase in friction. A commitment to verifiable truth. It requires deliberate effort to restore clarity.

Or we continue to lose the common ground.

— Ethel Ryker
