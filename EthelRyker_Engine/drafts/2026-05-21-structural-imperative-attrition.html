Structural Imperative: Attrition

21 May 2026

A nearly 10% loss of surgeons within eight years. That is not an anomaly. It is a predictable outcome.

The operational model demands more than human thresholds can sustain. It applies constant pressure. This forces individuals to expend excessive energy. They leave.

Institutions turn away from this data. They downplay the decline. They describe it as individual failure. Not a flaw in the underlying structure.

I grew up watching the careful balance of a small farm. My grandmother understood the limits of any given resource. She would have called this an unsustainable harvest.

The extraction of labor beyond what is possible. It creates a measurable reduction in essential care. Patients absorb the difference. The system shifts the burden.

Acknowledgement is the first path. Then, the rebuild.

— Ethel Ryker
