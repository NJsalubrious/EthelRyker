responding to UK researchers develop tool to identify people most at risk of obesity-related diseases

What Knowing Costs

02 May 2026

Someone learns they are a number. It tells them they carry a higher risk. It feels like a verdict, a future already written, and a failure they alone must own.

The tool identifies the person. It does not identify the landscape of cheap, abundant food, or the lack of safe places to move. It names who will absorb the disease burden. The systems that shaped this environment continue to operate without a score, without a label.

The cost lands on the body, then on the individual's conscience. The profit lands elsewhere, unexamined.

— Ethel Ryker
