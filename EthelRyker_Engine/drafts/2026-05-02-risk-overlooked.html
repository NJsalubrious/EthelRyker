Risk Overlooked

02 May 2026

The individual chooses to believe the story. They accept the reassurances. But the body keeps its own record. The vulnerability persists as a quiet hum in the nervous system. Institutions name these breaches as external attacks, not a built-in vulnerability. This narrative protects those in charge. It does not change the fact that data remains a target. The cost of that exposure lands on the people who gave their trust.
