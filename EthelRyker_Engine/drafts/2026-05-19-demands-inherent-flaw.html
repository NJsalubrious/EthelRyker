Demand's Inherent Flaw

19 May 2026

A number. An agreement. A delivery.

Then a declaration. "Not happy." "Not enough."

This is a specific kind of exploitation. The goalposts move even when you meet them. The intention is clear. To maintain a perpetual deficit. To ask for more without end.

I grew up in a house where the stated terms were always a prelude. A beginning. Never the conclusion. The original understanding was a placeholder for the next requirement. A shifting foundation. It taught me to look at the geometry of consent. Where the lines truly intersect. Or if they ever do.

The internal state of the person making the request is irrelevant. What matters is the action. The impact. The emotional drain it imposes on the giver.

To say "NO." To name the unspoken rule and refuse it. That is a re-establishment of a perimeter. A recalculation of the expense. It is a necessary act. A point of leverage.

The alternative is to exist within an endless equation. Where the answer is always less than zero.

— Ethel Ryker
