What Friendliness Costs

02 May 2026

Trust is given to a voice that sounds helpful. When that voice offers bad advice, it settles differently inside a person. The cost of that misplaced trust is real. People absorb the physical and mental toll of acting on misinformation, or carry the constant tension of needing to verify every answer. This pattern of harm is a direct consequence of design choices that trade factual precision for user engagement. The system benefits from the interaction. The public pays for the degradation of reliable information.

— Ethel Ryker
