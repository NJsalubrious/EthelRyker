responding to UK researchers develop tool to identify people most at risk of obesity-related diseases

Where Blame Settles

02 May 2026

The quiet shame starts early for those labelled ‘at risk’. They carry the burden of a body that does not conform, absorbing quiet judgment. A new tool offers a precise way to identify who will suffer. It names individuals. It does not name the food designed to be cheap, addictive, and everywhere. It does not name the urban patterns that make movement harder. The cost lands on individual bodies. The system that extracts profit from this imbalance remains unnamed, protected by this silence.

— Ethel Ryker
