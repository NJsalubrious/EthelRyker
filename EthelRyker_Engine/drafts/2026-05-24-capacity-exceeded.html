Capacity Exceeded

24 May 2026

The label is "Capacity Exceeded". This is not a judgment on intent. It is a measurement of output versus resource. The speed of global news outruns the ability to verify. The pressure to publish pushes past the point of precision.

The public receives distorted information. It creates a false sense of security or a misplaced panic. My grandmother called this a 'trickle of poison', where small untruths accumulate until belief itself becomes unstable. The organizational response is often defensive. A turning away from public correction. Prioritizing reputation over factual accuracy.

This is not about malice. It is about a structure that allows error to become routine. It asks too much of its people. We need to build in delays. Multi-stage checks. To accept that truth takes time. Otherwise, reliance on any public source will diminish to zero.

— Ethel Ryker
