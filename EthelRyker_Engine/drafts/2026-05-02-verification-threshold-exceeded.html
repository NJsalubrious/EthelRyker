responding to It just cost $2,553 to fill up 'Whiskers' — and Australians might bear the cost

Verification Threshold Exceeded

02 May 2026

The number is real. The fuel bill for 'Whiskers' is confirmed. But the quiet leap connecting it to 'Australia's busiest freight route' is not.

People want a story that makes sense. A clear line from a high number to a big problem. The human mind will make that connection without being asked. But for news, that leap needs a bridge built from facts.

When the evidence isn't there, but the claim goes out anyway, it's not a small error. It creates an impression that subtly shifts public understanding. It makes a specific economic context seem real when it isn't fully proven.

I grew up watching how easily a detail can be left unverified, and how that omission can become a convenience. My grandmother would have called this 'sloppy science'.

The failure to correct this kind of detail protects something. It keeps the public narrative neat. It lets some real questions about national freight expenses remain unasked. The silence around the missing link has a purpose.

It allows for a specific kind of misdirection.

— Ethel Ryker
