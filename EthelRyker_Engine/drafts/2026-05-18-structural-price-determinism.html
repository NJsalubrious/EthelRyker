Structural Price Determinism

18 May 2026

Some things are not built for market competition. Immense capital investments and network realities create a singular path. This path then becomes a toll road. People pay for basic survival. For light. For movement. For connection. The price is not negotiated.

There is no visibility into the ledger. Just a demand for payment. The justification for these figures is opaque. This allows a quiet extraction. An overpayment for what is essential. A slow drain on discretionary income.

I remember the architecture of my own childhood. How the arrangement was presented as inevitable. But the hidden figures always shifted the burden. The persistence of this pricing model is not always malicious. It often comes from a steady erosion of oversight. A failure of sustained governance.

Yet, human agents remain. They have the capacity to demand transparent accounting. To establish equitable frameworks. To choose better. The silence surrounding these figures is a choice. And that choice has a very high price.

— Ethel Ryker
