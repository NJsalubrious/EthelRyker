A boundary exists to define a space. A clear perimeter. When that line is repeatedly crossed, the intent is not mistaken. It is a declaration. A unilateral rejection of another's right to establish their own limits.

This dynamic is not an attempt at genuine connection. It is an assertion of dominance. A demand to occupy a chosen stillness. I recognise this structural arrangement. The design of absolute command.
