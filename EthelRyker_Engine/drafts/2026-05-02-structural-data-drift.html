Structural Data Drift

02 May 2026

People give their most personal information.
Their trust is the currency.
When genetic data, given for science, shows up for sale on Alibaba, that trust is broken.
The Biobank didn't sell it directly.
But the system allowed a researcher to take derived data and commodify it.
This was not unforeseen.
The minister expects it to happen again.
That means the door was left open.
It means capacity was exceeded.
It means the protections weren't resourced.
Blaming a single bad actor misses the point.
The system created the opportunity.
Repair means acknowledging the vulnerability.
It means fixing the enforcement.
The cost is paid by every person who now hesitates.
Trust is not replenished.

— Ethel Ryker
