Threshold Exceeded

15 May 2026

Thresholds are not advisory. They are structural integrity points. When a society allows prejudice to spread into daily life, its defensive barriers have failed.

The data is clear. This isn't abstract. It's the tangible reality of diminishing safety. The constant vigilance required to navigate a hostile climate extracts a physical and mental toll. It hollows out a person's core.

This turning away, this pretense of ignorance, is a specific kind of upkeep. It keeps uncomfortable truths out of sight. But the burden falls on those left exposed. I grew up where silence was presented as peace. It was just a different kind of violence.

The Australian civic frameworks that should have intervened stood silent. Perceived neutrality is a comfortable cloak for complicity. This wasn't a sudden rupture. It was a slow wearing down. A deliberate choice to not act.

— Ethel Ryker
