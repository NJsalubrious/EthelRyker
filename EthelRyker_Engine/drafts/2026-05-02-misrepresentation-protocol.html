Misrepresentation Protocol

02 May 2026

AI outperforms doctors. That’s what the headline promises.

It sounds like progress. Like a solution to something hard.

But the actual research doesn't say that. Not universally. Not yet.

They rush to be groundbreaking. They choose impact over precision.

Journalists are under pressure. The demand for content is constant. This is known.

But knowing the pressure does not absolve the choice to simplify. To exaggerate.

The system rewards speed. It rewards sensationalism. It doesn't reward careful vetting.

Who cops the cost? Anyone trying to understand real scientific advancement. Anyone who needs accurate medical information.

This false understanding creates distrust. It creates unrealistic expectations.

It sets up a public to believe something that isn't true.

That kind of misrepresentation is a risk.

Especially when patient safety is the final outcome.

— Ethel Ryker
