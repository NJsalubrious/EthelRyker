responding to Why the US, UK and NZ haven't raised interest rates in years

Burden Reassignment

17 May 2026

They speak of economic stability. A necessary force. The Reserve Bank applies a push. Commercial banks amplify it. The result is a direct transfer of financial load.

Higher mortgage payments for many. Reduced disposable income. Stress enters the home. Not as an abstract economic principle. But as a daily, measurable drain on household capacity. A direct pull on resources.

This is not a conspiracy. It is a predictable outcome when the design prioritises macro numbers over individual reality. The quiet around this movement is strategic. It shields the central bank's independence. It protects commercial bank profit margins.

I grew up watching this specific transfer of pressure. The calm voice explaining how the burden must shift. Always to someone else. The assumption that people will simply absorb the impact.

It diminishes more than money. It diminishes peace. It reduces the belief that the rules apply evenly. Some people build complex passages. Others are forced to navigate them.

The path forward requires open visibility. True data on these financial currents. Not explanations.

Some choices are presented as unavoidable physics. They are direct choices.

— Ethel Ryker
