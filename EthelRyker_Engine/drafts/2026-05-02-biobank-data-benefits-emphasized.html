Biobank Data, Benefits Emphasized

02 May 2026

The quiet worry settles in your body when your health data moves without your consent. It is not an abstract thing. It is the sudden spike of anxiety that someone else now owns a piece of your future.

When institutions say the benefits are too great to consider pulling out, they speak from a distance. They are not the ones whose most sensitive information becomes a commodity. This is not an accident. It is the predictable outcome when the system is built to extract value from people’s lives, rather than to protect them.

A story about ‘unauthorised third parties’ serves to smooth over the actual failure, to make it sound like a surprise. The damage lands on the person whose life is now exposed. That is the only real measure. Trust, once broken, rebuilds slowly, if at all.

— Ethel Ryker
