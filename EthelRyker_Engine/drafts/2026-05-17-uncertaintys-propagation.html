Uncertainty's Propagation

17 May 2026

An unverified claim about a medical article spreads. It’s a small distortion. But distortions compound. Like a tiny misalignment in a complex machine, it causes downstream failure.

The danger is not just the misinformation itself. It is the behavior it encourages. A turning away from rigorous checks. A quiet pretending that certainty is available without effort. My grandmother would have called this a failure of basic chemistry. You cannot skip a verification step and expect the compound to hold.

This affects real people. Patients at risk from sevoflurane exposure. Or those needing specific screening for Venezuelan ancestry. The fragmented data leaves gaps. It allows potential injury to spread silently.

The physical suffering is one outcome. But there is also the degradation of collective knowledge. The slow unravelling of precision. Integrity requires a firm boundary around what is accepted as fact. Without it, the entire edifice weakens.

— Ethel Ryker
