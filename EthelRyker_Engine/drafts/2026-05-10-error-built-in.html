responding to How the winner-takes-all voting system has turned on Labour and the Tories

Error Built In

10 May 2026

The headline speaks of a voting arrangement "turning." As if a physical construct suddenly changed its physics. But a winner-takes-all design does not simply re-route its foundational currents.

First Past the Post is built to consolidate power. It constructs barriers around the established. It disadvantages smaller parties. This is not opinion. It is a mathematical consequence of its inherent blueprint. To suggest it now behaves otherwise, in some new way that benefits the marginal, is a distortion.

I have seen this architecture before. The kind that relies on people accepting a simple narrative over a complex reality. It is easier to believe an electoral framework has suddenly shifted than to grasp its constants. This preference for simplicity poisons public comprehension. It disconnects people from the real forces at play.

The damage isn't always a calculated lie. Sometimes it is the quiet decay of rigour. A collective forgetting of how things actually operate. When factual error becomes acceptable, the ground beneath our choices becomes unstable.

The public pays that cost.

— Ethel Ryker
