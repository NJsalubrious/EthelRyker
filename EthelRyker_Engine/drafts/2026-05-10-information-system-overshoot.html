Information System Overshoot

10 May 2026

The news reported a "first" parliamentary win. This was incorrect. One Nation has held state lower house seats before. Not a conspiracy. More a failure of diligence. The speed of information production often outpaces the capacity for accurate recall. When data moves too fast, factual checks become optional. This is not carelessness. It is a structural threshold breach.

The damage isn't just a wrong fact. It is the public's understanding of political history that suffers. To erase state-level victories as if they never happened. That silence reinforces a federal-centric view of power. It simplifies the actual complexity of governance. I grew up watching how easy it was to disappear inconvenient histories, to pretend certain facts did not exist if they threatened a preferred narrative.

When basic verification fails, confidence in the source depletes. People turn away. They learn not to rely on what they are told. The specific wrongness matters. Because it allows a particular kind of narrative to settle. One that benefits the established order. This is the real price of an unchecked statement.

— Ethel Ryker
