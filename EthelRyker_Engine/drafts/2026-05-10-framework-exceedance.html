responding to Shell game: How Australian gas giants are using Singapore to reduce taxes

Framework Exceedance

10 May 2026

Frameworks exist for a reason. They define limits. When capital flows through a nation, it is meant to contribute to the collective structure. A simple exchange.

The mechanism is precise. Profits generated in Australia are sold at a low rate to shell companies in jurisdictions like Singapore. Services are purchased at inflated prices. This moves taxable income out of the country. Legally. But not without consequence.

The public absorbs the difference. Less funding for schools. Less for hospitals. The physical architecture of a society weakens. My grandmother would have called this a quiet theft. A turning away from the damage.

The rules governing this are outdated. Or they are deliberately ignored. A political calculus. The silence around it protects specific interests. The structure of wealth shifts. The threads of public connection thin.

The cost is always paid. Just not by the ones who designed the exit.

— Ethel Ryker
