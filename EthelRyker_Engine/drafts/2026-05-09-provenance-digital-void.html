Provenance: Digital Void

09 May 2026

Someone offered fake data for sale. They used a legitimate institution’s name to do it. This wasn't a breach of actual information. It was a confidence fraud.

The true injury is to public confidence. And the diversion of capital spent proving a lie never existed. This exact engineering of deception is familiar ground.

Digital marketplaces operate at a speed that outstrips their own validation. This makes them fertile ground for misdirection. The structural design allows for it. Platforms permitting 'black box' listings enable it. They create a vacuum where origin is assumed, not confirmed.

The path forward is clear. We need immutable digital provenance. So the source of data is undeniable. The people who build these deceptions must face consequences. The structure that enables them must change.

There is no other way to secure the perimeter.

— Ethel Ryker
