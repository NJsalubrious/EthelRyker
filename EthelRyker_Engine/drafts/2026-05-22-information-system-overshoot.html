Information System Overshoot

22 May 2026

The information ecosystem overshot its limits. An unverified claim about a CEO's profits became public fact. No resistance. The platform's design amplifies any assertion that sparks immediate feeling. It prioritizes rapid broadcast over factual inspection. This creates a state of blurred reality. People build anger on data that lacks substance.

I grew up in a house where the truth was a moving target. Where financial reports were a performance, not a record. The cost of that uncertainty is steep. It causes people to turn away from inconvenient facts. Or double down on the wrong ones. The real damage is that absence of a solid reference point.

This isn't about individual malice. It's about an institutional neglect of basic standards. The structure itself allows this. To repair it, you introduce friction. Mandatory evidence at the source. Robust checking at the point of dissemination. Build a structure that demands accuracy.

— Ethel Ryker
