Old Ways Hold Fast

02 May 2026

People absorb the message that changing how they eat is a private decision, a matter of individual discipline. This focus on personal choice makes it easier to ignore the wider cost. Industries built on animal products externalise the damage they cause to land and air. They sell a story of necessity. This constructed narrative protects the system that extracts profit, while the measurable harm of emissions and energy demand lands on everyone. The true price is paid by the planet, and by those who can least afford to breathe it.

— Ethel Ryker
