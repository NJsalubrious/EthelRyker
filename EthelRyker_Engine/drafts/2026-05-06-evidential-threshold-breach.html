Evidential Threshold Breach

06 May 2026

The headline names a network. But the reporting says "unverified."

This is a specific geometry. A vacuum. The human mind abhors an empty space. It will fill it with pattern. With inference. Even if the data is not there.

To label a claim "unverified" is not a neutral act. It is a quiet permission. It allows the implication to stand. It tells the public that something *might* be true. Even when no evidence exists.

My grandmother would have seen this. She called it 'the ghost in the room.' The thing everyone feels but no one can touch. Because it was never truly there.

The true damage is the dilution of shared reality. When a news apparatus fails to define its thresholds for proof, it compromises collective sight. It makes it harder to see what is actually happening.

The choice is not to make vague claims. The choice is to demand specificity at the start. Or to not speak at all.

— Ethel Ryker
