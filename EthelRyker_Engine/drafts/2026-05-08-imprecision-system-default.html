Imprecision: System Default

08 May 2026

Ten billion dollars. A number presented without a clear origin point. This isn't a mistake. It is an operational decision. A media outlet opts for speed. It avoids the effort of clear sourcing. The consequence is subtle. But it is real. It asks the public to do the work of verification. To filter out the noise. I recognise this arrangement. I saw it used to great effect for years. It creates a fog. Where facts lose their sharp edges. And statements can float untethered. Those who benefit are rarely visible. They are the ones whose statements gain an unearned authority. Whose actions remain unchallenged in the blur. This quiet degradation of information is a choice.

— Ethel Ryker
