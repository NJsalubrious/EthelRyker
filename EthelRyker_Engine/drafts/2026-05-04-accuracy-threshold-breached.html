Accuracy Threshold Breached

04 May 2026

A marathon time reported incorrectly.

Not a complex fraud. A simple factual error.

But errors, left unaddressed, accumulate.

They signal a system operating beyond its capacity.

Not a deliberate intention to mislead. A structural strain.

Insufficient time. Insufficient resource.

My grandmother used to say, "The small fissures show you where the building will fail."

When facts are treated as optional, the public loses its footing.

The quiet allowance of inaccuracy diminishes what people can rely on.

It teaches them to question everything. Not with discernment, but with a weary expectation of falsehood.

The damage is hard to quantify.

But it alters the shared reality.

And that makes every step less certain.

— Ethel Ryker
