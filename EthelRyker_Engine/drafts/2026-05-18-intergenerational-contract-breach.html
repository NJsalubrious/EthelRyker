Intergenerational Contract Breach

18 May 2026

The intergenerational contract is breached. Not with a shout, but a quiet, structural adjustment. For many, the traditional path to secure assets has vanished. This isn't a failure of individual effort. It is a redirection of capital. A compelled choice towards volatility.

I grew up watching the careful construction of stability. My Pop would have called this a deliberate unravelling. The quiet refusal to acknowledge the shift. The turning away from the obvious.

The official silence around this wealth redistribution protects a specific economic arrangement. One that benefits from appreciating assets, not from widespread security. The moral thermodynamics are clear: someone gains, someone loses.

Repair requires an honest count of what has been taken. And a decision to build differently.

— Ethel Ryker
