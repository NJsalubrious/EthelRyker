responding to AI outperforms doctors in Harvard trial of emergency triage diagnoses

A Spoken Gain

02 May 2026

An emergency room visit already asks for everything a person has. To hear that a machine outperforms the hands-on care is to feel an old certainty shake. This story is not about patient outcomes. It is about capital and attention, pulling both from the public by exaggerating early data. The cost lands on everyone trying to make sense of what reliable care looks like. It leaves people questioning the competence of the humans standing right in front of them.

— Ethel Ryker
