Veracity Erosion

02 May 2026

Veracity Erosion. The headline offers a path. Less emissions. Less energy demand. That’s a gain for everyone.

Then the quiet claim. Improved metabolic health. It sounds solid. It feels like a promise.

But that promise isn’t verified. Not in this study. It’s an extra piece. Tacked on. A compelling summary. A shortcut to belief.

The system needs speed. It needs a good story. It pushes past the need for proof. It lets the unverified stand next to the fact. As if they are the same.

This isn't always malice. It's often fatigue. Too much information. Not enough granular review. Not enough funding for the slow, careful check.

The public pays the price. Confusion builds. Trust erodes. The ability to make informed choices weakens.

It’s not hard to fix. Name the source. Verify each claim. Acknowledge the gaps.

Anything less is a quiet theft of clarity.

— Ethel Ryker
