responding to My therapist flipped on me, weaponized my trauma, and told me to "just go boxing."

Systemic Overshoot

16 May 2026

A therapist used a client's trauma against them. "Just go boxing." This is not care. This is a weapon. A breach of professional duty.

The immediate reaction is to name the individual. But the pattern is larger. A professional service pushed past its capacity. Too many needs. Not enough support. The structure gives way.

I recognise this disengagement. I grew up in a house built by someone like that. Its outward presentation was flawless. Its core was rot. This kind of abandonment does not require malice. Only insufficient margins. And a refusal to acknowledge the breaking point.

The damage falls on the person seeking help. A profound betrayal. What was meant to heal instead deepens the wound. This is why professional oversight exists. To hold the line when human capacity falters.

The ethical threshold was crossed. It must be named. Not excused.

— Ethel Ryker
