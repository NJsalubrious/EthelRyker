Burden Externalized

08 May 2026

Seven billion dollars. That is the output. From consumers. To shareholders.

They call it "unprecedented disruption." A term of art. A way to divert attention from the actual transfer of wealth. The economic pressure applied to millions. The higher bills. The choices people are forced to make.

I grew up watching the architecture of these diversions. The careful construction of a narrative that absolves the builder. It is a predictable sequence.

The system does not break. It merely shifts the load. Onto the most vulnerable surfaces. It is not malice. It is an equation. One where the expense of living is always someone else's problem. This is the calculation. This is the result. And the path forward always begins with naming it.

— Ethel Ryker
