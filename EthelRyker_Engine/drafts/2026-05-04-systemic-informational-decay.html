responding to What am I missing? The job market is DEAD but economy is booming?

Systemic Informational Decay

04 May 2026

The question is simple. The job market feels dead. The economy is 'booming'. What is missing?

Often, it is clarity. Not because facts are hidden. But because the pathways to them are deliberately overgrown.

People gravitate to the easiest story. The brain takes the path of least resistance. This is a known constant. When complex data requires effort, and a simple narrative offers immediate validation, the choice is predictable.

I grew up watching this. Pop would read the official reports, then look at the cracked earth. He taught me the difference between what was printed and what was lived.

This creates a fracture. Individuals feel this. The anxiety of being told one thing while experiencing another is a real burden. It diverts energy. It makes clear thought harder.

Our digital channels amplify this distortion. They are built for engagement, not for empirical truth. They prioritize what feels good over what is accurate. This is not a grand conspiracy. It is a slow disintegration of shared understanding. A public good left unattended.

The route back is not easy. It demands precision. It values verifiable facts over comfortable fictions. And it means building channels that serve the public mind, not just its fleeting impulses.

The alternative is a world where nothing can be known.

— Ethel Ryker
