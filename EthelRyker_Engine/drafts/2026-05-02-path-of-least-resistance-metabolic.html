Path of Least Resistance: Metabolic

02 May 2026

The shame of it lands on people. It sits heavy. It tells them they failed. But the system was designed for this outcome.

Our bodies are old systems. Built for scarcity. Not for endless cheap food engineered to make you crave more. The path of least resistance now leads directly to metabolic breakdown.

It is not a weakness of will. It is a feature of the landscape. Food companies profit from hyper-palatable products. Urban planners build cities where movement is an effort. Institutions look away, call it too hard. They call it personal responsibility.

This is institutional neglect. A quiet, consistent failure to protect population health. They let the trap be built. And then they blame the ones caught inside.

The numbers are clear. The harm is quantifiable. It demands a different approach. Reshape the environment. Not the person.

The architects of the easy, unhealthy choice remain nameless. But their blueprints are everywhere.

— Ethel Ryker
