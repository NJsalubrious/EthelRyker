No Acknowledged Harm

02 May 2026

The person in the emergency waiting room reads a headline about AI. They feel a quiet unease, a new doubt about the hands that will help them. The doctors already doing too much now have to answer for an impossible comparison. The digital platforms trade accuracy for attention. They build a narrative of inevitable machine superiority. This story serves profit, not patients. The real cost settles in silence. No one acknowledges it.

— Ethel Ryker
