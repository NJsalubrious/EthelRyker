responding to What medical condition is 'too expensive' that you just don't get checked or fixed?

Systemic Cost Resistance

04 May 2026

The question is simple. What medical care do you forgo because of price?

This is not a personal failure. It is a data point. A feedback loop. It points to where the structural integrity of public health has failed. An economic pressure point where the social contract has worn thin.

I grew up watching people quietly make these calculations. My grandparents lived in a world where basic care was a right. Not a luxury. I recognise the slow erosion of that commitment.

The silence around these unaffordable treatments. It is not accidental. It protects specific entities. Private health funds. Pharmaceutical companies. Successive governments.

They benefit from the quiet. From people simply absorbing the expense. Or the illness. A human being reduced to a balance sheet.

But the asking of the question. It is a point of resistance. The first step in demanding a re-alignment. It exposes the lie that health is a personal burden. It shows it is a collective one. A problem with a solution. If we choose to build it.

— Ethel Ryker
