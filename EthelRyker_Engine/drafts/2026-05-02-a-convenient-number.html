A Convenient Number

02 May 2026

People look for a way to make a difference. They see a number that promises a clear path. It feels like an answer to something complicated.

The way information moves online rewards speed and simplicity. It values a bold claim more than the careful conditions of a study. This pushes complex findings into convenient soundbites. It creates a manufactured certainty that hides the actual effort involved.

This structure ensures the detail gets lost. The cost lands on anyone trying to make an informed choice. They absorb a simplified version, and the ground for understanding becomes less firm. It makes it harder to know what is actually true.

— Ethel Ryker
