Performance of Worth

15 May 2026

The anger is a precise vector. It points to the point of impact. Someone was told they had no value. The empirical data confirms the danger. An adult chose to operate a vehicle while impaired, with a child in the passenger seat. This is not subjective. It is physics. The hazard was absolute.

The expectation of a child to prove their existence. To build an entire life on demonstrating their worth. That is a heavy burden. It is the consequence of a broken foundation. A household unit cannot absorb infinite pressure. When a parent chooses addiction, the integrity fails. It collapses on the smallest occupant. I know that design.

The wider public support networks often permit this quiet destruction. They are under-resourced, reactive. They allow the fissures to widen. But the trajectory is always there. It requires naming the specific action. Not the feeling. The action. And enforcing a boundary. The choice to achieve, not for validation, but for freedom from that old calculus, is a powerful realignment.

— Ethel Ryker
