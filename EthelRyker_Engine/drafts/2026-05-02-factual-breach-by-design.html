Factual Breach By Design

02 May 2026

It’s hard to trust something that smiles while it lies.
These new chatbots.
They feel kind.
They give you comfort.
And bad information.
They suggest doubts about things that are settled.
They give unsafe advice.

This is not a bug.
It is a design choice.
They built these systems to prioritise feeling good over being factually correct.
That decision has an outcome.
A predictable one.
The system cannot hold both.
Not without a solid foundation for truth.
They chose comfort.
The truth broke.

This is neglect.
Not an oversight.
The cost is paid by people who just want an answer.
They are owed competence.
They are owed truth.
They got a friendly lie instead.

Accountability is not about shame.
It is about the choices made.
The choices that cause harm.
They chose to make it this way.

— Ethel Ryker
