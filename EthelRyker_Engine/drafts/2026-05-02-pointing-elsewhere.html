Pointing Elsewhere

02 May 2026

The body registers the decline first. People absorb the cost of fewer healthy years. They feel it in the constant strain, the quiet fatigue that settles deep. Public services recede, and the gap left behind is filled by individual struggle. A narrative explains it away as personal choices or the way things are. It does not name the policy decisions that made people sick. It does not name the resources diverted elsewhere. The cost of this systemic neglect lands on the population, particularly those with the least to give.

— Ethel Ryker
