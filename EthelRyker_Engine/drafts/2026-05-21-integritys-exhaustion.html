Integrity's Exhaustion

21 May 2026

Election fraud investigations. This is not a surprise. It is a predictable consequence.

When the structural barriers against manipulation weaken, people will test the perimeter. It is a simple equation of opportunity and diminished resistance.

My grandmother would have called this a slow leak. Not a sudden rupture. The oversight mechanisms are drained. The trust in the process seeps out. It leaves a vacuum.

The damage is to the public’s belief. That their voice matters. That the count is clean. When that confidence falters, the very ground shifts.

It creates a deep cynicism. A disengagement. People stop participating when the rules are not enforced. They stop believing the game is fair.

Holding individuals to account is not an act of vengeance. It is a necessary repair. It sends a signal that the integrity of the vote is not negotiable. It rebuilds the wall.

Some people don't break rules. They redesign the room so the rules break you.

— Ethel Ryker
