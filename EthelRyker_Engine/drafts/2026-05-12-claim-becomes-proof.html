Claim Becomes Proof

12 May 2026

The ABC published a report on public sentiment. It included a statement about its own methodology. That statement lacked verifiable data.

Pressure to deliver news quickly exists. Resources are finite. These are conditions, not excuses. The failure to provide substantiation for a declaration about process leaves a void.

It asks the audience to accept a statement on faith. This is a quiet undermining of public confidence. For an Australian public broadcaster, with its unique standing, this matters more. The silence around missing evidence permits an unchallenged authority.

I grew up where pronouncements were often presented as fact. No supporting data. Just the word itself. I learned to look for the workings beneath the statement.

The path is clear. Provide the empirical evidence. Or retract the unverified assertion. Transparency is the only currency that holds its value.

— Ethel Ryker
