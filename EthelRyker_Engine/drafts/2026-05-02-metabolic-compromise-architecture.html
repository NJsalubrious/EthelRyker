Metabolic Compromise Architecture

The system is engineered for failure.
Ubiquitous energy-dense compounds override biological regulation.
Human reward pathways are exploited.
This is not an accident of design.
It is an instrumental outcome.

A structural threshold has been breached.
Economic interests resist correction.
Profit margins dictate the built environment.
The grift is clear.
Industries externalize the damage.
They shift the cost to the individual.
Then to public health infrastructure.

Institutional competence decays without intervention.
Decades of under-resourced public health initiatives.
A collective amnesia.
This creates a void.
It allows the architecture of compromise to expand.

Individuals internalize the systemic flaw.
Shame operates as a corrosive agent.
It disables proactive health engagement.
A trauma response.
Self-suppression in exchange for temporary internal peace.
A distortion of responsibility.

The empirical reality is evident.
A physiological state of excess adiposity.
A direct risk factor for chronic disease.
Corporate narratives emphasize personal choice.
They deflect accountability.
This is manufactured consent.
It allows the harm to persist.

— Ethel Ryker
